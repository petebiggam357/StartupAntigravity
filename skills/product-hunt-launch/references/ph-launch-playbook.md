# Ethical Product Hunt Launch Playbook (T-14 to T+7)

## Hard Product Hunt Submission Specs

| Asset / Field | Limit / Dimensions | Best Practice |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Product Name** | `<= 40` characters | Clean brand name only — no tagline stuffed into the name |
| **Tagline** | `<= 60` characters | Concrete outcome + ICP; zero buzzwords (`revolutionary`, `AI-powered`, `best`) |
| **Description** | `<= 260` characters | Clear 2-sentence explanation of problem solved + Secret Sauce |
| **Topics** | Max **3** topics | Pick 1 broad + 2 tight high-intent categories |
| **Thumbnail** | `240 × 240 px` (PNG/GIF) | High-contrast logo mark, readable at tiny feed size |
| **Gallery Images** | `1270 × 760 px` (4–6 slides) | 1. Outcome Hero, 2. 3-Step Workflow, 3. Live UI Screenshot, 4. ROI Proof/Comparison, 5. Transparent Pricing + PH Offer |
| **Demo Video** | 60–90 seconds (YouTube/Loom) | Show real working product in the first 5 seconds; founder voiceover beats flashy motion graphics |

## 14-Day Countdown Timeline

### T-14 to T-7 (Build Momentum Ethically)
- [ ] Publish your **Product Hunt Coming Soon** teaser page and share the link in your email signature, LinkedIn, and X bio.
- [ ] Make a list of **50–100 real warm contacts** (pilot users, discovery interviewees, FI mentors/cohort peers, FAST advisors) who already know your work.
- [ ] Prepare your `1270x760` gallery images in Canva or Figma and verify your live landing page / Stripe Checkout works end-to-end.

### T-3 to T-1 (Final Lock)
- [ ] Schedule launch for **12:01 AM PT** (Tue–Thu for peak B2B traffic; Sat–Sun for higher odds of #1–#3 Product of the Day with less competition).
- [ ] Double-check `PH_LAUNCH_KIT.md` character counts and links.
- [ ] Pre-write 3 authentic social posts (story of why you built it, lessons learned, live build transparency).

### Launch Day (24 Hours Starting 12:01 AM PT)
- [ ] **12:02 AM PT:** Post your **First Maker Comment** immediately.
- [ ] **06:00 AM – 09:00 AM PT:** Send personal, warm notes to your supporter list inviting honest feedback and questions (never say *"please upvote"* — say *"we're live on Product Hunt today and would love your honest feedback in the comments"*).
- [ ] **All Day:** Reply to every comment within 15 minutes; turn thoughtful commenters into live onboarding calls or Paid Pilot PACTs.
- [ ] **Zero Dark Patterns:** Never use fake countdown timers, upvote-exchange groups, or scraped spam blasts.

### T+1 to T+7 (Convert Attention Into Profit)
- [ ] Follow up personally with every new signup and commenter within 24 hours.
- [ ] Capture verified customer quotes and conversion numbers in `.startup/interviews.json` & `.startup/pacts.json`.
- [ ] Add your Product Hunt traction stats (signups, paying conversions, badge if earned) as `[VALIDATED]` proof to `pitch-deck/deck.json` and your next `docs/INVESTOR_UPDATE.md`.
