# Product-Market Fit Playbook (Sean Ellis × Superhuman × Founder Institute)

## The 4-Question Sean Ellis / Superhuman Survey (Send Verbatim)

Send to active users who have experienced your core product at least twice (takes 90 seconds to answer):

1. **"How would you feel if you could no longer use [Product]?"**
   - `Very disappointed`
   - `Somewhat disappointed`
   - `Not disappointed`
2. **"What type of person do you think would most benefit from [Product]?"**
   - *(Reveals your High-Expectation Customer — HXC — in your customers' own exact words!)*
3. **"What is the main benefit you receive from [Product]?"**
   - *(Reveals your true value prop — often sharper than what founders assumed.)*
4. **"How can we improve [Product] for you?"**
   - *(Feeds the 50/50 roadmap ONLY from `very_disappointed` and high-fit `somewhat_disappointed` users.)*

## Objective PMF Decision Matrix (Zero Wishful Thinking)

| Signal | Pre-PMF / Pivot Risk | Approaching PMF (Narrow ICP) | Proven PMF (Scale Engine) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Sean Ellis Score (`Very Disappointed`)** | `< 25%` across all segments | `25% – 39%` overall (or 1 segment `>= 40%`) | **`>= 40%`** across core ICP (n >= 20+) |
| **Retention Curve** | Decays steadily toward 0% | Stabilizing in narrow niche | **Flattens cleanly** + NDR >= 100% |
| **Willingness to Pay & Pull** | Heavy discounting / favors only | Repeat pilots & renewals | Organic inbound & unprompted referrals |
| **Required Founder Action** | **STOP scaling.** Re-interview buyers or pivot ICP/problem. | Cut low-fit segments; run 50/50 roadmap for HXC segment. | **Green light** to scale GTM (`gtm-and-cash-velocity`) & raise Seed! |

---

## Common PMF Illusions to Reject Immediately

1. **"People signed up for our free waitlist!"** — Free signups are curiosity, not PMF. Only paid retention and high `very_disappointed` scores count.
2. **"Investors love our deck!"** — Investors are not your customers. Real customer pull beats investor applause every time.
3. **"We just need more marketing spend!"** — Pouring ad dollars into a `< 25%` PMF leaky bucket accelerates bankruptcy. Fix retention and narrow your ICP first.
