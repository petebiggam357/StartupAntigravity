# Founder Institute Funding Readiness & Profit Benchmarks

| Stage | Product & Validation State | Monthly Revenue (MRR) | Growth & Unit Health |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Idea** (`idea`) | FI One-Sentence Pitch passes linter; 15+ Mom Test interviews | \$0 | Avg pain >= 4/5; clear willingness-to-pay |
| **Validation** (`validation`) | Riskiest Assumptions tested; paid pilot / pre-order PACTs signed | First Dollar (\$1–\$1K) | Written Customer PACTs before heavy build |
| **FI Accelerator / MVP** (`mvp`) | Live vertical-slice MVP with active paying users | **\$1K – \$10K MRR** | 0–20% MoM growth; >70% gross margin |
| **FI Pre-Seed Traction** (`pre_seed_traction`) | Production product + repeatable acquisition loop | **\$10K – \$25K MRR** | 10–20%+ MoM growth; LTV:CAC >= 3:1; payback < 6 mo |
| **FI Seed & Scale** (`seed_scale`) | Early Product-Market Fit + low churn + scaling GTM | **\$25K – \$200K+ MRR** | Strong retention; Default-Alive option unlocked |

## High-Profit North Star & AARRR Guardrails

Track one **North Star Metric** tied directly to customer ROI + one **For-Progress iKPI**:
- **Acquisition:** Qualified ICP prospects entering pipeline per week
- **Activation:** % of new customers reaching First Value inside 48 hours
- **Retention:** Monthly logo retention (>= 95% SMB, >= 98% mid-market) & Net Dollar Retention (>= 105%)
- **Revenue:** Net New MRR + Gross Profit Dollars + Cash Conversion Cycle
- **Referral:** Organic customer-to-customer referrals (proof of ethical delight)
