# Bottom-Up Market Sizing & Defensible Moat Reference

## Bottom-Up Sizing Formula

Never use top-down analyst reports alone. Always construct:

- **TAM** = `Total Target Buyers Globally (or Target Geo) × Realistic Annual Contract Value (ACV)`
- **SAM** = `Subset of TAM reachable by your current product, language, compliance & channel`
- **SOM** = `Realistic customer count you can close in Years 1–3 × ACV`

### Primary Buyer Count Sources
- US Census County Business Patterns (NAICS codes) / BLS
- LinkedIn Sales Navigator / Apollo / Clay headcount & title counts
- Public regulatory/licensing registries (NPI healthcare, DOT carriers, state bar/CPA boards)
- Ecosystem marketplaces (Shopify apps, Salesforce AppExchange, GitHub orgs)

## 6 Real Startup Moats (Secret Sauce Audit)

1. **Proprietary Data Flywheel**: Every customer workflow improves accuracy/speed for all future customers (with ethical consent).
2. **Workflow System-of-Record Switching Cost**: Embedded directly into daily revenue or compliance operations.
3. **Distribution Wedge**: Unique access to an underserved community, channel partner, or product-led viral artifact.
4. **Structural Cost Advantage**: 10x cheaper unit delivery via automation/architecture that legacy vendors cannot match without cannibalizing services revenue.
5. **Network Effects**: Product gets measurably more valuable as more participants join.
6. **Trust & Mensarius Brand Moat**: Radical transparency and customer-owned data in industries plagued by predatory incumbents.
