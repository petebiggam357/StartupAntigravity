# Exponential Thinking, Singularity & ExO Architecture

*(Educational adaptation inspired by Singularity University (`su.org`), Ray Kurzweil's Law of Accelerating Returns, Peter Diamandis's 6Ds & Abundance frameworks, and Salim Ismail's Exponential Organizations — not an official Singularity University release.)*

This skill pairs **10x Moonshot Vision** with **Startup Antigravity's 7–14 day First-Dollar & Mensarius Oath discipline** — so founders dream at Global Grand Challenge scale while executing with ruthless unit-economics clarity.

## 1. Massive Transformative Purpose (MTP) + FI Grounding
- Craft a crisp, aspirational **MTP** (5–12 words) anchored to a **Global Grand Challenge** (energy, health, water, food, education, governance, prosperity) and mapped to your **UN SDG iKPI** (`ikpi_catalog_search`).
- **Anti-Hype Gate:** An MTP inspires talent, community, and investors — it NEVER replaces a concrete **FI One-Sentence Pitch** (`fi_pitch_lint`) or paying Customer Pilot PACTs.

## 2. The 6Ds of Exponentials (`exponential_audit(action="six_ds_map")`)
Map where your target industry sits on Peter Diamandis's 6Ds curve to sharpen your *Why Now* investor narrative:
1. **Digitized** — Analog workflow becomes digital data.
2. **Deceptive** — Early doubling looks tiny (`0.01 -> 0.02 -> 0.04`); incumbents dismiss it. *(Best time to build with early adopters!)*
3. **Disruptive** — Knee of the curve: 10x better/cheaper suddenly overtakes legacy incumbents.
4. **Demonetized** — Marginal cost collapses toward zero (e.g., LLM inference + `x402` USDC micropayments).
5. **Dematerialized** — Physical overhead vanishes into software & autonomous agents.
6. **Democratized** — Once-elite capabilities reach billions globally.

## 3. Exponential Organization (ExO) Blueprint (`exponential_audit(action="exo_scorecard", write_doc=true)`)
Score how a tiny 2–5 person team achieves 10x output without linear headcount:
- **SCALE (External Abundance):** **S**taff on Demand, **C**ommunity & Crowd, **A**lgorithms (AI/MCP agents), **L**everaged Assets (cloud/APIs), **E**ngagement.
- **IDEAS (Internal Control):** **I**nterfaces (automated onboarding/APIs), **D**ashboards (Startup Command Center), **E**xperimentation (Riskiest Assumptions), **A**utonomy, **S**ocial Technologies.
- Generates `docs/EXPONENTIAL_BLUEPRINT.md` with your **ExO Quotient (0–100)** and top 3 leverage multipliers.

## 4. Law of Accelerating Returns & Singularity Horizon (`exponential_audit(action="accelerating_returns_sim")`)
- Ray Kurzweil's **Law of Accelerating Returns** shows information technologies improve exponentially in price-performance.
- Model how 2x–5x annual AI inference cost drops transform today's prototype margins over 12, 24, and 36 months — while still using tiered model routing & caching today (`ai_x402_calc`) so you protect the **>=75% gross margin floor**.
- **Human-Agency & Alignment Ethics (Mensarius Oath):** Exponential leverage magnifies both good and harm — keep human-in-the-loop approvals on irreversible financial/clinical/legal actions, reject dark patterns, and measure real societal progress via your iKPI.
