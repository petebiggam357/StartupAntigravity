# Founder Institute FAST Agreement Reference

## Standard FI FAST Equity Matrix (2-Year Vesting, 3-Month Cliff)

| Engagement Tier | Monthly Commitment | Idea Stage | Startup Stage | Growth Stage |
| :--- | :--- | :---: | :---: | :---: |
| **Standard** | ~2 hrs/mo (monthly call + email) | **0.25%** | **0.20%** | **0.15%** |
| **Strategic** | ~5 hrs/mo (bi-weekly + warm intros) | **0.50%** | **0.40%** | **0.30%** |
| **Expert** | ~10 hrs/mo (weekly + recruiting/sales) | **1.00%** | **0.80%** | **0.60%** |

## Advisor Onboarding Checklist
- [ ] Run a 30-day unpaid "work trial" (1–2 real working sessions) before offering equity
- [ ] Define 3 specific quarterly deliverables in writing
- [ ] Confirm 24-month vesting with 3-month cliff and IP assignment clause
- [ ] Review official FI FAST Agreement template (`fi.co/fast`) with startup counsel
- [ ] Log advisor role on Slide 10 (`Team & Advisors`) of `pitch-deck/deck.json`
