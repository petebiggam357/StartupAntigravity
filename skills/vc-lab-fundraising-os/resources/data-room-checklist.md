# VC Lab & Pre-Seed/Seed Data Room Checklist

## Essential Data Room Folders

1. **`01-Pitch-and-Overview/`**
   - Latest `pitch-deck/index.html` exported to 16:9 PDF (clean mode — `B` key toggled off)
   - `docs/ONE_PAGER.md` (with FI One-Sentence Pitch & Lean Canvas)
   - `docs/ETHICS_AND_IKPI.md` (Mensarius Ethical Charter & For-Progress iKPIs)
2. **`02-Financials-and-Metrics/`**
   - 18-month financial & runway model (base / conservative / optimistic)
   - Live MRR, gross margin, churn, CAC, LTV, and Cohort retention table
   - Current Cap Table + SAFE dilution summary
3. **`03-Customer-and-Traction-Proof/`**
   - Anonymized Customer Discovery summary (15+ interviews, pain & WTP stats)
   - Signed Customer Paid-Pilot PACTs / LOIs / Case Studies (strip raw PII!)
4. **`04-Corporate-and-Legal/`**
   - Certificate of Incorporation & Bylaws
   - Founder IP assignment + 4-year vesting schedules + 83(b) filing proof
   - Signed FI FAST Advisor Agreements

> **Subject:** Intro: [Company Name] — [10-word traction or problem-solution hook]
>
> Hi [Connector], thanks so much for offering to connect us with [Investor Name] at [Fund] — their thesis on [specific fund thesis] is a tight fit.
>
> **[Company Name]** is developing [offering] to help [specific audience] [solve problem] with [secret sauce].
> - **Traction:** [$X MRR / X paying pilots / X% MoM growth — verified]
> - **For-Progress iKPI:** [1-line measurable impact metric]
> - **Round:** Raising [$X] on a [$Y cap] SAFE ([$Z] already soft-circled)
>
> Deck link: [URL]
>
> Would love a 15-min chat if it fits their thesis!
