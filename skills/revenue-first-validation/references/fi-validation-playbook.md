# Founder Institute & Mom Test Validation Playbook

## Bad Questions vs. Mom Test Questions

| Leading / Bad Question (Produces Polite Lies) | Unbiased Mom Test Question (Produces Truth) |
| :--- | :--- |
| "Do you think this is a good idea?" | "Walk me through the last time this problem cost your team money." |
| "Would you pay $99/mo for a tool that fixes this?" | "How much are you paying today for your current workaround or agency?" |
| "What features would you want us to build?" | "What have you already tried to fix this, and why did you stop using it?" |
| "Would you use this every week?" | "Show me the exact spreadsheet/tool you opened this morning to handle it." |

## One-Page Customer Paid-Pilot PACT Template

Inspired by Decile Group's PACT clarity — replaces vague "keep me posted!" replies with concrete commitment:

```markdown
# Founding Partner Paid Pilot PACT

- **Customer:** [Company / Buyer Name]
- **Provider:** [Your Company Name]
- **Core Deliverable:** [Single measurable outcome delivered within 14 days]
- **Pilot Fee:** $[Amount] (50% founding-partner discount; full refund if Core Deliverable is not met)
- **Target Kickoff Date:** [YYYY-MM-DD]
- **Ethical Guarantee:** Customer retains 100% ownership of their data; cancel anytime with zero hidden fees.
```

## Kill / Pivot / Persevere Rubric

- **Persevere (Move to MVP):** Pain >= 4/5 across 15+ interviews AND >= 2 paying customers or signed Paid-Pilot PACTs.
- **Pivot ICP or Offer:** High pain (>= 4/5) but buyers lack budget or authority — move up-market to the budget holder or sharpen the ROI promise.
- **Kill Fast:** Pain < 3/5 after 15 interviews and zero budget allocated to workarounds today. Save months of your life and test a sharper problem.
