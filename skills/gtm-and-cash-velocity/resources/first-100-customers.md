# First 100 Paying Customers — Ethical Playbook

## High-Converting Ethical Cold Outreach Template (Zero Spam)

**Subject:** Quick question on [specific workflow] at [Company]

> Hi [First Name],
>
> Saw [specific public signal — e.g., hiring for X / recent launch / industry compliance change].
>
> We built [Company] specifically for [exact ICP] to [quantified outcome, e.g., cut manual claim errors by 80%] using [Secret Sauce].
>
> Open to a 10-minute look at how we do it — or I can send a 90-second Loom showing it on a sample workflow? Zero pressure either way.
>
> — [Founder Name]

## Launch Week Checklist (Show HN / Product Hunt / Communities)

- [ ] Landing page hero passes FI Clarity Check (10-word headline, clear ICP, zero buzzwords)
- [ ] Live 90-second product walkthrough video or interactive sandbox ready
- [ ] Transparent pricing + instant Stripe Checkout / Paid Pilot booking live
- [ ] 15+ early supporters / beta users personally invited to share honest feedback (never buy fake upvotes or reviews!)
- [ ] Analytics tracking Activation -> Conversion -> Day-7 Retention (not just pageviews)
- [ ] Founder calendar cleared for 48 hours to answer every single comment and onboard users live
